Use **Workspaces** for your organisation’s Teams agreement. **Subscription** in the account hub covers your individual plan.

The owner and anyone granted **View billing** can open the organisation’s invoices. Sign in, choose **Workspaces**, select the organisation and open **Billing → Workspace invoices**. Choose **View invoice** for the relevant charge. Accounts payable can also use the invoice sent to the billing email on the agreement. [Open workspace billing](https://superseeded.ai/account?section=Workspaces)

## What your organisation pays for

For a new annual agreement or renewal, the workspace owner opens **Workspaces → Billing → Payments due**, reviews the agreement and selects **Pay in Stripe**. Checkout shows the amount due in Australian dollars, including applicable GST. The agreed term becomes available once payment succeeds and the start date arrives. Closing Checkout before paying leaves the agreement unpaid. Return to **Billing** to try again.

Stripe returns you to the organisation’s workspace. When payment is confirmed, choose **View paid invoice** in the confirmation. You can also open **Workspace invoices** later to download the invoice and receipt. The invoice identifies the payment date, amount and tax.

| What happens | Effect on your invoice |
| --- | --- |
| An administrator creates an invitation | The invitation uses no seat and creates no charge. |
| An employee accepts and a paid seat is free | The employee takes that seat. There is no additional invoice. |
| An employee accepts when all paid seats are occupied | An additional seat requires prior spending approval from an authorised administrator. A colleague with seat purchasing permission pays in **Billing**. The invited employee gains access once payment succeeds. |
| An employee is removed | Their paid seat becomes available for another employee through the end of the term. The annual seat charge continues. |

For example, if **Members** shows **8 of 10 seats**, two more colleagues can join using seats already paid for. Capacity is checked when each colleague accepts. When creating an invitation, someone with **Authorise seat purchases** can approve one additional seat if needed. The invitation form shows the estimated charge and the contract end date. The actual fee is the agreed annual seat rate prorated for the time remaining when the seat is reserved.

## Change billing details or query a charge

Contact SuperSeeded to change the billing email, legal name, ABN, billing address, purchase order reference or commercial terms. Include the workspace name, the change and when it should take effect. For an invoice correction or payment reconciliation, include the invoice number and, if already paid, the payment date and reference. Send sensitive payment details only through the payment channel specified on your invoice. [Contact SuperSeeded](mailto:hi@superseeded.ai)

## Earlier invoice agreements

If your organisation already has an agreement with invoice payment terms, follow the due date and instructions on its invoice. Under those agreements, shared access is suspended when an outstanding invoice reaches 30 days past its due date. A colleague with billing access can review outstanding invoices. Contact SuperSeeded with the invoice number if a payment needs to be matched.

## Renew, pause or end the agreement

- **Renewal.** Renewal requires a new agreement for the next term. Contact SuperSeeded before the current end date with your expected seat count, billing contact and next purchase order reference. If the next term has fewer seats than you need, review the member list with your workspace owner.
- **Temporary pause.** The owner can choose **Pause workspace access** and later **Resume workspace access**. A pause preserves shared files and the billing agreement continues.
- **End of term or early cancellation.** Contact SuperSeeded about your agreement and effective end date. Before access ends, download the project versions and attachments your organisation needs. Contract expiry preserves shared content and closes shared access.
- **Permanent deletion.** **Delete workspace** removes member access and starts deletion of shared content. Your billing agreement continues. Settle contract changes with SuperSeeded separately and retain invoice copies for your records before deleting the workspace.

## Pay for workspace AI

The annual agreement specifies how many compute credits members share for that term. After payment, the allowance becomes available at the start of the term and expires at its end. Once available credits are spent, Studio pauses requests that use workspace credits.

In **Workspaces → Billing**, the owner can buy additional Studio compute credits for the selected organisation. One credit costs US$0.10 before applicable tax. After Stripe confirms payment, the workspace receives the credits and the owner can open the paid invoice.

Members share this balance when their chosen provider is set to **Workspace compute credits**. Studio checks available credits before sending the request. A provider set to use a company or personal key bills the holder of that key directly.
