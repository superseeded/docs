Open **Workspaces** in the account hub and choose your organisation. Under **Members**, the first number is the number of people occupying seats. The second is the number of seats your organisation has paid for. [Manage workspace members](https://superseeded.ai/account?section=Workspaces)

## Choose a role

| Role | What the employee can do by default |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Manage the contract’s invoices and seat purchases, members, custom roles, shared files, AI settings and workspace keys. The owner can transfer ownership, pause access or delete the workspace. |
| **Admin** | Invite and manage members within their authority, manage shared files and configure workspace AI providers and company keys. Seat purchases and invoice access require separate permissions. |
| **Contributor** | Open shared files and publish new files or project versions when shared storage is enabled. |
| **Viewer** | Open and download shared files and project versions when shared storage is enabled. |

For a finance colleague who needs invoice access, the owner can create a **Custom role**, select **View billing**, choose **Save role** and assign that role to the colleague. To delegate paid invitations, include **Manage members** and **Authorise seat purchases**. Each member has one role. Include all the permissions they need when replacing an existing role.

## Invite a colleague

1. Under **Invite a colleague**, enter their work email and choose their role.
2. If you have spending authority, decide whether to authorise one extra seat if all paid seats are occupied when they accept. Review the estimate before approving it.
3. Select **Create invitation link**. Copy the link and send it to the intended employee. They must sign in with the verified email you invited. The link is single-use and expires after seven days.

If an open invitation went to the wrong address, choose **Cancel invitation** and create a new one. If an employee is asked for spending approval, you can free an existing seat or replace the open invitation with one that includes approval. If the message refers to an upcoming contract, ask the owner to review the renewal seat count.

## Change responsibilities or offboard someone

Change the role beside the member’s email to adjust their permissions. For an ownership handover, first add the incoming owner as a member. The current owner chooses **Transfer ownership** beside the incoming owner’s email and confirms. The outgoing owner becomes an **Admin** and can then be removed if they are leaving.

Choose **Remove member** to revoke future workspace access and free the seat. Follow your organisation’s offboarding process for files already downloaded to the employee’s device. If they held an integration credential, review or replace it separately. Workspace API keys belong to the organisation and can continue to work after a member leaves. Members with audit permission can choose **View workspace activity** to review recent administrative actions.
