---
title: "Get started with Studio Teams"
description: "Set up your organisation’s workspace, prepare for the first project and help colleagues sign in."
---

Your organisation’s Teams workspace gives colleagues access to SuperSeeded Studio under one annual agreement. The owner manages the workspace in the account hub. Designers use the same SuperSeeded sign-in in the desktop app and select the organisation they are working for.

## Set up your organisation

1. **Confirm the agreement.** Give your SuperSeeded contact the workspace name, the intended owner’s SuperSeeded account, your legal business name and the accounts payable email. For an Australian agreement, confirm the billing address, Australian Business Number (ABN) if applicable, and any purchase order reference. Agree on the start date, annual fee, included seats, additional seat rate and payment terms.
2. **Choose how project files will be handled.** Tell your contact whether colleagues may share files using managed Australian storage or must keep files locally. If a client requires a particular processing location, backup period or deletion timeframe, agree on those requirements before uploading its project.
3. **Review AI access before inviting designers.** External AI is on by default, including for a workspace with local-only file storage. The owner or an authorised admin can turn it off or select the providers and keys employees may use. [Configure project privacy and AI](/studio-teams/projects-and-privacy)
4. **Invite the first colleague.** Once SuperSeeded has provisioned the workspace and the contract term has begun, sign in to the account hub, open **Workspaces** and select your organisation. Use **Invite a colleague** to create a link for their verified work email. [Open the account hub](https://superseeded.ai/account?section=Workspaces)

## Join as an employee

Open the invitation link from your workspace administrator. Sign in with the invited email address, verify it if prompted and choose **Accept workspace invitation**. If the invitation has expired or was addressed to another email, ask the administrator for a new link.

In a Teams-enabled version of SuperSeeded Studio, sign in with that same account and select your organisation under **File → Workspace**. If you belong to more than one organisation, check the workspace name before opening or sharing a project. If the organisation is missing, check the account and invitation in the account hub, then ask the owner to confirm the contract has started.
